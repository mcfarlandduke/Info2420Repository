Welcome to Native Cooking of Utah

Discover the authentic flavors of Utah cuisine. Our collection of recipes celebrates the rich culinary heritage of Utah, from Bear Lake Raspberry Ice Cream to classic Green Jello desserts.

Featured This Season

Master Chef Pierre DeBois invites you to explore the simple, inexpensive, and delicious recipes that make Utah cooking unique. Whether cooking for a large family gathering or an intimate dinner, our recipes are designed to impress.

  • Inexpensive ingredients

  • Feeds large amounts of people
