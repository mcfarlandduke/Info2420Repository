Native Cooking of Utah - Color Palette

Design Philosophy

The color scheme for Native Cooking of Utah was chosen to evoke warmth, comfort, and traditional home cooking. The palette uses earthy, natural tones that reflect the rustic charm of Utah's culinary traditions.

Primary Colors: Warm browns and oranges represent home-cooked meals and the warmth of Utah hospitality.

Background: Cornsilk provides a soft, inviting canvas that is easy on the eyes for extended reading.

Text: Dark slate gray ensures excellent readability while maintaining the organic feel.

Accessibility Notes

All color combinations have been tested for sufficient contrast to ensure readability:

  • Body text (#2F4F4F) on background (#FFF8DC): High contrast ratio
  • H1 headings (#8B4513) on background: Excellent readability
  • H2 headings (#D2691E) on background: Good contrast
  • Links (#B8860B) on background: Sufficient contrast
  • All text elements meet WCAG accessibility guidelines
